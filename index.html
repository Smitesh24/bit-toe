How it works

Survivor rules

  1. It’s a standard 3×3 board. X moves first in round 1.
  2. The board never resets. Pieces stay on it from one round to the next.
  3. Round win: one random piece from the winning line survives and the others are removed. If one move completes two lines, one piece survives from all of them. The loser moves next.
  4. Round draw (full board, no line): 2 random X and 2 random O are eliminated. Turns keep alternating.
  5. Survivors count toward future lines, so an old piece can help win a later round.
  6. Most round wins after 7 or 13 rounds takes the match. The match ends early once the lead can’t be caught.
  7. Tied after the final round? Overtime: the next round anyone wins decides the match.
